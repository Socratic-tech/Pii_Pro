How it works
1. Add spreadsheet

Upload a CSV. The page checks column names and samples values for common privacy risks.

2. Review identifiers

Direct identifiers are flagged, possible identifiers are marked for review, and free-text fields get extra attention.

3. Verify the result

Compare before and after, confirm the remaining fields, then download your cleaned copy.

Important: This helper can detect common risks, but it cannot determine that a dataset is FERPA-compliant or safe for every use. You are still the final reviewer and should follow your district's policies.
