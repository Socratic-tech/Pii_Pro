👋 Welcome! Here's how this works:

  1. Upload your spreadsheet: Drag and drop your CSV file here, or click "Choose File" and then "Load My File".
  2. Choose what to do with private info:
    • Remove columns — Student names, IDs, and contact info? Gone!
    • Hide the info — Keep the columns but replace sensitive stuff with "***"
    • Fun pseudonyms — Want to call students "Sunny Falcon #1" and "Pixel Otter #2"? Check that box! (Makes it easier to discuss students without using real names)
    • Pick extra columns to remove — See something you don't need? Just check the box.
  3. Take a peek: Click "Preview" to see before and after — first 5 rows so you can double-check everything looks good!
  4. Save your clean file: Click "Download Clean File" and you're done! You'll get a safe-to-share version plus a log file showing what changed.
💡 First time here? Don't worry! This tool is designed to be simple. Just load your file and it'll automatically spot things like student names, emails, and phone numbers. You can always preview before downloading anything.
🔒 Important Note About Privacy: This tool helps protect student privacy, but you're still responsible for making sure your cleaned data follows FERPA and your district's policies. The tool removes obvious private info, but please review the results before sharing. If you're not sure, check with your data privacy coordinator!
