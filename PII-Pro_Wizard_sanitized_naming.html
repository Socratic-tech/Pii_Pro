👋 How it works
1
Upload your CSV

Drag and drop your file or click "Choose File." The tool automatically spots student names, IDs, emails, and other private info.

2
Choose how to handle private info

Remove columns entirely, mask values with ***, add fun pseudonyms like "Swift Falcon #1", or pick extra columns to drop.

3
Preview your changes

See the first 5 rows before and after — double-check everything looks right before downloading.

4
Download clean files

You'll get your sanitized CSV plus a log file showing exactly what changed — great for your records.

🔒 Privacy reminder: This tool removes obvious private info, but you're still responsible for FERPA compliance and your district's data policies. Always review results before sharing, and check with your data coordinator if unsure.
